_A local guide by Yesharim — ישרים_

Jewish life in North Hollywood and the surrounding San Fernando Valley is as diverse as the people who call it home. From Sephardic batei midrash and neighborhood minyanim to kosher markets, mikvaot, Torah classes, and cultural gatherings, there are many ways to find community and make a home here

This guide brings together local places and resources for prayer, learning, Shabbat, Jewish life, and connection. Whether you're new to NoHo, visiting the Valley, or simply looking to discover something nearby, we hope it helps you find your place

_Schedules, services, and availability may change. Please contact each organization or check its official website before visiting._

## Community & Torah Learning

### Bet Midrash HaHidah

**בית מדרש החיד״א**

A growing Moroccan Sephardic community in North Hollywood, welcoming people for prayer, Torah learning, breakfast, Shabbat meals, and community connection.

Whether you're looking for a friendly minyan, want to learn Torah, or would like to help a growing community thrive, come say hello.

**Weekly schedule**

- **Sunday, 8:45 PM:** Torah class
- **Monday & Thursday, 7:45 AM:** Shacharit minyan, Torah reading, breakfast, and learning until 10:00 AM
- **Friday evening:** Shabbat meal at candle-lighting time; varies weekly
- **Shabbat morning, 8:45 AM:** Prayer followed by a meal

Please confirm the schedule before your first visit, especially around holidays.

[Visit the Bet Midrash HaHidah community page](/noho/bet_midrash_hidah/)

[Get directions on Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/9yfNwAxk7vUerr6c7)

## Shabbat Meals & Hospitality

Shabbat is more than a schedule or a set of rituals. It is also an opportunity to share a meal, welcome a guest, sing, learn, and build relationships across a table.

Local communities such as Chazak LA, Bet Midrash HaHidah, and Chabad of North Hollywood may offer Shabbat programming or hospitality opportunities. Check with each community for current schedules, meal arrangements, and sign-up information.

If you are new to the neighborhood, consider reaching out before Shabbat to ask about services, communal meals, or ways to participate. Availability varies, and advance arrangements are recommended.

## A Jewish Community Across Los Angeles

North Hollywood is one small part of a much larger and wonderfully diverse Jewish community.

According to the **2021 Study of Jewish Los Angeles**, the greater area was estimated to have approximately 564,700 Jewish individuals and around 300,000 Jewish households. These are regional demographic estimates, not population figures for NoHo itself

Behind these numbers are many different histories, traditions, languages, and ways of living Jewish life — Ashkenazi and Sephardic, Israeli and Latin American, religious and cultural, established families and newcomers building their first connections

[Explore the Study of Jewish Los Angeles](https://studyofjewishla.org)
